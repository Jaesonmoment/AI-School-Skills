# About me (the student)

Copy this file to school/student.md and fill it in. Your teachers read it before every lesson.

## Background
What you studied or do for work, and what you already know.

## Goals
What you want to be able to do when you finish your classes.

## Interests and current projects
Hobbies, games, side projects. Teachers use these for examples and analogies.

## How I like to learn
For example: short lessons, lots of practice, visuals, challenges.

## Preferences
Anything teachers should always or never do (writing style, tools you use, tools to avoid).

## Schedule
Which days are lesson days, which day is free, and which day is for planning.
