# Changelog

The version is the one in `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`. An update changes only the skills: your `school/` folder, your house rules and your subject packs stay as they are.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-06)
- House rules: `school/house-rules.md` holds your rules for every teacher, outside the skills, with a template in `skills/school-teacher/`.
- Subject packs: an optional extra skill for one subject (course map, trusted sources, tool setup, conventions, misconceptions, exercises) that plugs into the teacher, with a template in `skills/school-teacher/`.
- Pick what you need: every skill works on its own, says what to do when another one isn't installed, and the README lists which skills to install for each use.
- This changelog.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-06)
- Every session opens with a refresher (a recap of last time plus warm-up questions) without the student asking, longer after a gap, in every role.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-05)
- Three roles per class: study helper, full teacher, work checker.
- A new class starts with research, then a placement check in rounds, before any task.
- New `subject-research` skill.
- Light mode for students with limited usage.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-05)
- The single teacher skill split into ten focused skills, with `school-teacher` as home base.
- Rule: research and write the plan before giving any task.

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-05)
- Class format: in-person classroom or online class.
- Big categories: how to split a large subject into separate classes.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-17)
- `ai-school-setup`, the admissions counselor.
- A Claude Projects guide in the README.
- Rule: solve the problem as given.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-17)
- First release: the school teacher skill, as a Claude and Codex skill and a Claude Code plugin.
